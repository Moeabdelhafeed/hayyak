Our Process

A structured letting process

Every leasing instruction follows a structured four-stage process — designed to minimise void periods and maximise the quality and security of income placed in each asset.

01
Market Assessment

Rental market analysis, comparable evidence review, and competitive positioning — establishing the right asking rent and letting strategy before launch.

02
Active Marketing

Targeted marketing across Hayyak Property Management's occupier network and referral channels — supplemented by digital and direct marketing as required.

03
Tenant Selection

Rigorous qualification of shortlisted tenants — financial standing, covenant quality, business profile, and fit with the asset's income and occupier mix objectives.

04
Lease & Handover

Negotiation, heads of terms, legal instruction, lease execution, and structured handover to Hayyak Property Management's team — ensuring continuity from first enquiry through to active tenancy.
